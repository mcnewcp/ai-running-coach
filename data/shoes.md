## Replacement guideline

Typical trainer lifespan is **300–500 miles** — it's the midsole foam that fatigues (loses cushioning/rebound), not the upper or outsole looks. **For Coy specifically, lean conservative — replace at ~300–400 mi, or at the first sign of new aches or a "flat" feel** — because worn cushioning transmits more impact to the patellar tendon, a variable worth controlling tightly during/after the rehab block.

Replace-now signals (beyond mileage): new knee/shin/hip aches, midsole creasing that doesn't rebound, outsole worn smooth at push-off/heel, or the shoe simply feeling dead. Keep rotating 2+ pairs — foam decompresses between runs (extends total life) and gives comparison data if one pair feels better on the knees.

Proactively flag a pair here as it approaches ~300 mi.
