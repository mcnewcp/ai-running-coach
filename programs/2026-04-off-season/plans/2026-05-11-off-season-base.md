# Off-Season Base Phase — May 11 to Jul 19, 2026

Ten-week aerobic base rebuild between the Music City Half (Apr 25, 2026) and the speed block beginning Jul 20. See [current-goal.md](../../../data/current-goal.md) for the full off-season phase map.

## Goals

- Rebuild and expand aerobic capacity post-race
- Progressively grow weekly volume from ~14 mpw → 25 mpw
- Reintroduce HSR 2x/week, restore tendon load tolerance
- Keep everything easy — no tempo, no intervals, no hill repeats
- Reawaken neuromuscular system with strides (Week 2+)
- Establish a 5K baseline time (no current PR on the board) in early June
- Arrive at the speed block (Jul 20) healthy, fit, and ready to absorb intensity

## Framework Targets

- **Mileage**: Build 14 → 25 mpw across 10 weeks, ~10%/week ramp, cutback every 4 weeks (-20-25%)
- **Frequency**: 4 runs/week ramping to 5 runs/week mid-phase
- **Intensity**: 100% easy / Z2 (HR <150). The only non-Z2 work is strides (neuromuscular, not aerobic) and the early-June 5K time trial.
- **HSR**: 2x/week on **Monday + Thursday**, post-easy-run
- **Strides**: 4-6 x 20s, 1-2x/week starting Week 2, after easy runs
- **Long run**: Saturdays. Never exceed 35% of weekly volume.
- **Sauna**: 4x/week, 20 min
- **Mobility**: 1-2x/week standalone, plus pre/post-run routines

## Weekly Structure

Standard week template — adjust day-by-day per energy, weather, knee status:

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Easy run + HSR |
| Tue | Easy run (or rest, early weeks) |
| Wed | Easy run |
| Thu | Easy run + HSR |
| Fri | Rest / mobility / sauna |
| Sat | Long run |
| Sun | Rest |

**Rules**:
- HSR on Mon/Thu so Fri can be a full lower-body recovery day before Saturday's long run
- No consecutive hard days (HSR counts as hard load)
- Easy runs stay strictly Z2 — HR ceiling 150, lower the better
- If knees flare >3/10 for 2+ days running, reduce volume and reassess

| Day | Plan |

**Total**: 14 mi, 4 runs, 2 HSR (reintroduction load)

**HSR reintroduction note**: After ~2.5 weeks off, restart at ~70% of prior working weight for Week 1, ~85% Week 2, full working load Week 3. Protect the patellar tendon during the restart window.

## 5K Time Trial (Week 4 — early June)

The first benchmark of the off-season. Replaces the Saturday long run in Week 4 (Jun 6).

- **Purpose**: Establish baseline 5K time (no current PR on the board) to anchor the speed block
- **Execution**: Run as a hard solo effort — measured course or treadmill, ~15-20 min warmup + 5K hard + 10 min cooldown
- **Pacing**: First mile slightly conservative, build through mi 2, race mi 3
- **Not a race**: Just data. Knees rule first.

## Phase Markers

- **End of Week 4 check-in**: Volume tolerance, knee status, HR/pace trend, 5K result
- **End of Week 7 check-in**: Pre-cutback assessment; ready for Week 8 cutback?
- **End of Week 10**: Final base assessment; arrive at speed block healthy

## Red Flags

Same as always: knee pain trending up, easy-run HR drifting up without explanation, sleep degrading, motivation flatlining. Flag any of these immediately — base phase has zero need for forced volume.

## Transition Out (Jul 20)

Speed block begins. Introduce VO2max intervals and threshold work. Drop one easy day to fit the workout days; mileage holds or rises slightly to 25-28 mpw. New phase plan file: `2026-07-20-speed-block.md`.
