## Guiding principles

1. **Volume before intensity.** Re-establish a consistent easy-mileage base first; intensity is a small seasoning on top (still ~80/20).
2. **Strides before tempo.** Weeks 1–2 add strides only. Light tempo enters ~Week 3, one session/week, and only if the tendon stays quiet.
3. **Still no hills, intervals, or dedicated downhill** — the rehab plan's "hold off until 4+ consecutive weeks of full training volume with no symptom flare" rule carries over. Gentle rolling terrain is fine now.
4. **Early-morning running is the default** through the summer heat (the block's hard-won lesson — 6am → HR 148 vs midday 151–169).
5. **Strength continues** on the six-pattern framework, 3×/week. The rehab guardrails relax: the 3s squat descent is now optional (keep it if you like it), and the decline squat test drops to **biweekly / as-needed** monitoring rather than mandatory weekly.
6. **Sauna 4×/week**, mobility, sleep — unchanged.

## Intensity Reintroduction Protocol

### Strides (Weeks 1+)
- **What:** 4–6 × ~15–20 sec at ~mile-effort (smooth and fast, NOT sprinting), full walk-back recovery (~60–90s).
- **When:** tacked onto the end of an easy run, 2×/week (Tue + Thu).
- **Where:** flat ground, good footing. No hills.
- **Why:** reintroduces leg speed and elastic loading at low volume/low risk — the gateway back to intensity.

### Light Tempo (Week 3+)
- **Start:** one continuous block of **~10–12 min at "comfortably hard"** (~half-marathon-ish effort, HR ~160–165, conversational-in-short-phrases). Full easy warm-up + cool-down around it.
- **Progression:** extend the tempo block ~2–3 min per exposure (→ 15, then 18–20 min) as it stays comfortable and the knee stays quiet. One tempo session/week, on the freshest-leg day (Tue).
- **Rule:** if the knee gives *any* uptick (during or next-morning) after a tempo run, drop back to strides-only for a week and retest.

## Strength (six-pattern framework continues)

## Monitoring / Red Flags (carried from rehab block)

- Knee pain trending up week-over-week, or any pain >3/10 during running → back off intensity first, then volume.
- Elevated easy-run HR in *cool* conditions (a fatigue/illness tell — see the Aug 6 vs Aug 9 lesson).
- Tempo that produces next-morning tendon soreness → strides-only reset.
