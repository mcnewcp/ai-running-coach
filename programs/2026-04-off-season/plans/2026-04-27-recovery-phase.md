# Recovery Phase — Apr 27 to May 10, 2026

Two-week reverse taper after Music City Half (Apr 25, 2026). First phase of the off-season program. See [current-goal.md](../../../data/current-goal.md) for the full off-season phase map.

## Goals
- Allow musculoskeletal recovery from race effort, especially patellar tendons
- Maintain aerobic touch without accumulating training stress
- Preserve sauna/recovery routines
- Reset for off-season base building beginning May 11

## Framework Targets
- **Mileage**: 0 → ~15 mpw across two weeks
- **Intensity**: All easy, conversational, HR <150
- **Frequency**: 3–4 short runs per week, no consecutive run days early on
- **Cross-training**: Swim and mobility encouraged
- **Sauna**: 4x/week, 20 min sessions
- **HSR**: Held until base phase begins May 11 (full 14-day tendon reload)
