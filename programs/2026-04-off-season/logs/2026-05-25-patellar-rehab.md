### Lift sessions

**Mon May 25 — Lift A** (~16:30, post-work)

| Slot | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Working loads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leg press (3s/3s) | 3 × 15 | 140 → 160 → 160 lb |
| 2 | Reverse lunge w/ DBs (3s/3s), lead left | 3 × 15/leg | 2× 15 lb → 2× 12.5 lb → 2× 10 lb |
| 3 | 45° hyperextension (2s/1s) | 3 × 15 | BW → +10 lb → BW |
| 4 | Single-leg hip thrust, lead left | 3 × 8/side | BW |
| 5 | Banded lateral walks | 2 × 15/dir | yellow band |

- First session of the block — pure calibration of 15RM working loads.
- **Slot 1 (leg press)**: 160 lb felt like the right 15RM ballpark; next Lift A try 160–170.
- **Slot 2 (reverse lunge)**: weights dropped 15→12.5→10 as the 6-sec tempo accumulated — true 15RM with this tempo is likely **2× 10 lb** or **2× 12.5 lb**. Use 2× 10 lb across all 3 sets next time and reassess.
- **Slot 3 (hyperextension)**: +10 lb plate on set 2 → back to BW on set 3 suggests fatigue, not load. BW × 15 with strict hinge is fine for Week 1; add the 10 plate consistently in Week 2.
- **Slot 4 (single-leg hip thrust)**: BW × 8 was the easy end of the 8–12 range — add a light DB on hip next session if knees stay clean.
- **Slot 5 (lateral walks)**: yellow band ✓ — pick the band that lets you push knees out without form breakdown.
- No in-lift knee pain reported. ✓
