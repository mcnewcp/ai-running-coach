**Hill Repeats Guidance (Wednesday)**:
- Location: Vivrette Smith Park (steep trail) OR moderate paved incline
- Warm-up: 10-15 min easy running
- Workout: 6-8 x 60-second uphill efforts
  - Effort: Moderately hard (not sprinting, controlled)
  - HR will rise (~160-165 bpm acceptable)
  - Focus on form: lean slightly forward, drive knees, powerful arms
  - Recovery: Jog slowly down the hill (full recovery before next rep)
- Cool-down: 10 min easy jog
- Knee Note: Downhill jogging easy and controlled (minimize impact)
