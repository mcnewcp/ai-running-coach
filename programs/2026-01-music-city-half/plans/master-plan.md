## Weekly Training Structure

### Standard Training Week Template

**Monday**: Easy Run (30-45 min, Zone 2) + HSR Session + Sauna

**Tuesday**: Easy Run (30-45 min, Zone 2) + Mobility + Sauna

**Wednesday**: Workout Day (tempo/hills/progression, 45-60 min total)

**Thursday**: Easy Run (30-45 min, Zone 2) + HSR Session + Sauna

**Friday**: Rest OR Easy Run (30 min, Zone 2) + Sauna (if resting)

**Saturday**: Long Run (progressive build, see weekly plan)

**Sunday**: Active Recovery (walk or complete rest) + Mobility (20-30 min) + Sauna (optional)

### Weekly Targets Throughout Training

**Running**:
- 4-5 runs per week
- 1 long run (Saturday)
- 1 workout day (Wednesday, starting Week 3)
- 2-3 easy runs
- 0-1 rest days

**Strength & Conditioning**:
- 2 HSR sessions (Monday/Thursday preferred)
- Progressive plyometric loading (as detailed in hsr-protocols.md)

**Recovery**:
- 4 sauna sessions per week (20 min each)
- Daily mobility work (pre-run dynamic warm-up, post-run stretching)
- 1 standalone deep mobility session per week (20-30 min)

## Phase Breakdowns

### Phase 1: Base Building (Weeks 1-6)

**Objective**: Build aerobic foundation, establish Zone 2 discipline, strengthen tendons

**Key Focus Areas**:
- Zone 2 heart rate discipline (<150 bpm on easy runs)
- Progressive mileage building (12 → 19 mpw)
- Long run extension (5 → 8 miles)
- Consistent HSR training for knee health
- Establish mobility routine

**Workouts**:
- Weeks 1-2: Easy running only, focus on HR discipline
- Week 2: Add strides (4-6 x 20s pickups after easy runs)
- Week 3: First progression run (last 10 min slightly faster)
- Week 5: Easy hill repeats (6-8 x 60s uphill, jog down recovery)
- Week 6: First tempo run (20 min steady at moderate effort)

**Success Criteria**:
- Consistent Zone 2 compliance on easy runs
- Knee pain maintained at <3/10 average
- Complete all planned mileage
- No injuries or setbacks

### Phase 2: Build (Weeks 7-11)

**Objective**: Increase volume, introduce race-specific tempo work, progressive long runs

**Key Focus Areas**:
- Continue Zone 2 discipline while adding intensity
- Build weekly mileage (20 → 27 mpw)
- Long run progression (9 → 11 miles)
- Tempo runs at threshold effort
- Hill repeats for strength-endurance
- Maintain injury prevention practices

**Workouts**:
- Week 7: Tempo run (25 min steady)
- Week 8: Hill repeats (8-10 x 90s uphill)
- Week 9: Tempo run (30 min, approaching HM pace)
- Week 10: Cutback week (easy running only)
- Week 11: Progressive long run (last 3 miles at HM effort)

**Success Criteria**:
- Build to 27 mpw without injury
- Complete 11-mile long run confidently
- Knee remains manageable (<3/10 average)
- Successfully integrate tempo and hill workouts

### Phase 3: Peak (Weeks 12-14)

**Objective**: Solidify fitness, practice race pace, peak volume

**Key Focus Areas**:
- Peak weekly mileage (28-32 mpw)
- Race-specific pace work (HM pace runs)
- Final long run preparation (up to 13 miles)
- Maintain recovery practices
- Mental preparation for race

**Workouts**:
- Week 12: HM pace run (5 miles at goal pace)
- Week 13: Tempo run (35 min) - PEAK VOLUME WEEK (32 mpw)
- Week 14: Final long run (13 miles: 10 easy, last 3 at HM pace)

**Success Criteria**:
- Complete peak volume week healthy
- Execute 13-mile long run successfully
- Gain confidence in HM pace
- Body responding well to training load

### Phase 4: Taper (Weeks 15-16)

**Objective**: Recover, maintain sharpness, arrive at start line rested and ready

**Key Focus Areas**:
- Volume reduction (35% in Week 15, 60% in Week 16)
- Maintain some intensity (shorter tempo work)
- Prioritize sleep and recovery
- Mental preparation and race logistics
- Trust the training

**Workouts**:
- Week 15: Tempo run (20 min) - reduced volume
- Week 16: Easy shakeout runs only (2-3 miles max)

**Race Week Protocol**:
- Monday/Tuesday: 3-4 mile easy runs
- Wednesday: 20 min tempo (race week sharpener)
- Thursday: Rest or 2-mile shakeout
- Friday: Complete rest or 15-min shakeout
- Saturday: RACE DAY
- Sunday: Recovery walk or rest

## Training Principles & Guidelines

### 80/20 Training Distribution

**80% Easy Running** (Zone 2):
- Heart rate <150 bpm
- Conversational pace (can speak full sentences)
- Builds aerobic base, mitochondrial density
- Allows high volume without excessive fatigue
- Forms foundation of all training phases

**20% Hard Running**:
- Tempo runs (lactate threshold, ~15K-HM pace)
- Hill repeats (strength-endurance, controlled effort)
- Progressive long runs (race-specific practice)
- NO consecutive hard days
- Always follow with easy run or rest

### Load Management & Injury Prevention

**Weekly Mileage Increases**:
- Standard increase: ~10% week-over-week
- Cutback weeks: Every 3-4 weeks, reduce 20-30%
- Never increase >15% without cutback

**Long Run Guidelines**:
- Never exceed 35% of weekly mileage
- Progression: Add 0.5-1.0 mile every 1-2 weeks
- Cutback weeks: Reduce long run proportionally

**Knee Pain Protocol**:
- **0-3/10**: Proceed as planned
- **4-6/10**: Modify (reduce distance, treadmill, easy pace only)
- **7+/10**: Rest, consider medical consultation
- Always prioritize knee health over planned mileage

### Recovery Integration

**Sauna Protocol**:
- 4x per week, 20 minutes per session
- Post-workout (within 2 hours) or standalone
- Not immediately before hard workouts
- Benefits: Recovery, blood pressure management, heat adaptation

**HSR Training**:
- 2x per week, non-consecutive days
- After easy runs or on rest days from running
- Progressive loading throughout 16 weeks
- See hsr-protocols.md for detailed progression

**Mobility Work**:
- **Pre-run**: 5-10 min dynamic warm-up (every run)
- **Post-run**: 5-10 min stretching/foam rolling (minimum 3x/week)
- **Standalone**: 1-2x/week, 20-30 min deep mobility
- See mobility-routines.md for specific protocols

### Zone 2 Heart Rate Training

**Target HR**: <150 bpm on all easy runs

**Why It Matters**:
- Builds aerobic base (mitochondrial density, capillary development)
- Improves fat oxidation
- Allows high training volume without overtraining
- Speed comes from improved aerobic efficiency over time

**Common Challenges**:
- Feels slow initially (this is expected)
- Requires patience and trust in the process
- May need to slow pace to keep HR in zone

**Success Metric**: Running faster at same HR over time

## Monitoring & Adjustments

### Weekly Check-ins

**Review Every Week**:
- Total mileage vs. planned
- Knee pain trends
- Zone 2 compliance
- Sleep and recovery quality
- Energy and motivation levels

**Adjust If**:
- Knee pain >3/10 consistently
- Chronic fatigue (may indicate overtraining)
- Illness or injury
- Life stress impacting training

### Monthly Progress Checks (Every 4 Weeks)

**Assessment Points**:
- Week 4 (end of base phase 1)
- Week 8 (mid-build phase)
- Week 12 (entering peak phase)
- Week 16 (race week)

**What to Evaluate**:
- Fitness progression (optional 5K time trial or pace assessment)
- Knee health status and trends
- Mileage tolerance
- Recovery adequacy
- Confidence level
