## Disney Week Modifications

**Why modified**: Staying at the Polynesian Village Resort at Disney World all week. Key adjustments:

1. **Park walking volume**: Park days add 7-12 miles of walking on hard surfaces daily (15,000-25,000+ steps). This is real training load that replaces some easy run volume.
2. **No sauna access**: Skip for the week. One week off won't impact BP management or recovery.
3. **Limited gym**: Resort fitness center likely has dumbbells and basic machines. HSR adapted with available equipment.
4. **Heat/humidity**: Central Florida in February is warmer/more humid than Nashville. Expect HR to run 5-10 bpm higher at same effort. Run by feel, accept slower paces.
5. **Running routes**: Resort paths along Seven Seas Lagoon (Polynesian → Grand Floridian loop, ~2.5-4 mi). Treadmill available as backup, especially for tempo run.

**Approach**: Keep the two key workouts (tempo run + long run) as the Phase 1 capstone. Trim easy runs to account for park walking load. Prioritize early morning runs (6-7am) before parks when temps are 55-65°F.

- HSR Sessions: 1 (adapted with available equipment)

**Modified HSR Options (Monday - use what's available)**:
- Goblet squats (heavy dumbbell): 4 × 8, slow 3-second eccentric
- Dumbbell split squats: 3 × 8/leg
- Single-leg step-ups (bench or box): 3 × 10/leg
- Bodyweight eccentric decline squats: 3 × 10, 4-second eccentric (if no decline board, use a folded towel under heels)
- Single-leg deadlift with dumbbell: 3 × 8/leg

**Tempo Run Guidance (Wednesday)**:
- **Recommended: Resort treadmill** for controlled conditions (avoid humidity impact on HR)
- Warm-up: 15 min easy running (Zone 2)
- Workout: 20 minutes steady at threshold effort
  - Effort: "Comfortably hard" (can speak 3-4 words at a time, not full sentences)
  - Pace: Likely ~10:30-11:00 min/mile on treadmill; if outdoors, go by feel not pace (heat will slow you)
  - HR: ~155-165 bpm (will drift upward, that's OK)
  - Purpose: Lactate threshold development, mental toughness, race-specific effort
- Cool-down: 10 min easy jog + 5 min walk
- Mental Note: This is NOT a race. Find a sustainable effort you could hold for 30-40 min if needed.

**Long Run Guidance (Saturday - 7.0 miles)**:
- Repeat of last week's distance (smart given cumulative park walking fatigue)
- **Early morning strongly recommended** (6-7am, before parks, cooler temps)
- Route options: Resort path loops along Seven Seas Lagoon, or treadmill
- Stay in Zone 2 (accept slower pace if warm/humid)
- Break it mentally into chunks (e.g., 2-mile segments)
- **Bring water and a snack** - lesson learned from Week 5's 7-miler
- HR target: <150 bpm, but allow for heat adjustment (up to 155 is OK in warmth)
- If running outdoors, hydrate extra the day before
