## Pacing Strategy — "Finish Strong"

**This is the playbook based on your training**:

### Miles 1-3: HOLD BACK
- Target pace: **10:45-11:00/mi**
- HR ceiling: <155
- Adrenaline + downhill streets + crowd will make you want to fly. **DO NOT.**
- Treat this as your warm-up — you didn't get one.

### Miles 4-9: SETTLE IN
- Target pace: **10:30-10:45/mi** if feeling good
- HR target: 150-160
- This is the "boring middle." Run by feel + HR, not pace.
- Conversational effort still — should be able to say short sentences.

### Miles 10-13.1: DECIDE
- **If you feel good**: Gradually pick up effort. Pace may drift to 10:00-10:30. HR can climb to 165.
- **If you're managing**: Hold the pace, finish steady. No heroics.
- Final 0.5 mile: empty the tank only if you have it.

### Hard rules
- ❌ **Do NOT chase any specific time.** The goal is "finish strong and healthy."
- ❌ **Do NOT race anyone in the first half.** Let people pass — you'll catch them later.
- ✅ **Walk through every aid station** (10-15 sec) to drink fully without choking.
- ✅ **If knee pain spikes >5/10**: ease off, finish at conversational pace.

**Reference points from training**:
- HM-distance training (4/11): 13.16 mi @ 10:34 pace, HR 150
- HM-pace run (3/25): 5 mi @ 9:51 pace, HR 162
- Recent easy runs: 10:30-11:00 at HR 138-149

A realistic "finish strong" race likely lands around **2:15-2:25** total time. Anchor on effort, not the watch.

## Contingencies

**If knee pain >6/10 mid-race**: ease to easy pace, finish under control. No heroics for a finish-strong-and-healthy goal.

**If cough flares badly during run**: walk breaks, slow down, finish at survival pace. Don't DNF unless something serious — a finish line crossing matters for the goal.

**If weather is hot (>70°F at start)**: drink at every aid station, slow target paces by 5-10 sec/mi, take it easier early.

**If weather is cold/wet**: add throwaway long-sleeve, wear cap, accept slower pace.

**Course time limit**: 4 hours timing-tag time. You'll finish in ~2:15-2:25, so this is a non-issue.
